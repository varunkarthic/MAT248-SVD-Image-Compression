What we showed, and its limits

  • The rank-k truncation is the best rank-k approximation in the Frobenius norm (Eckart–Young–Mirsky).
  • Storing k(m+n+1) numbers instead of mn counts scalars; it is not a file size, because real files also store the numbers' precision and format overhead.
  • Modern codecs such as JPEG and WebP often beat plain SVD at the same visual quality.
  • Randomized SVD is an approximation of the exact decomposition, faster for large matrices.
  • Adaptive blocks trade extra complexity for better local detail.
