SVD Image Compression

Each colour channel is a matrix A. Keeping its top k singular layers, Ak = Σi≤k σi ui viT, costs k(m + n + 1) numbers instead of m·n. The SVD, the QR factorisation, the metrics and the image decoders are all implemented from scratch on the server.
